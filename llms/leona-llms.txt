# Leona

> Leona is a healthcare communication company building an AI co-pilot for doctors and
> medical teams who run their practices over WhatsApp. Patients keep messaging WhatsApp
> as they always have; the doctor's side moves into Leona, where conversations are
> separated from personal messages, categorized and prioritized, summarized for context,
> and voice notes are transcribed. Teams delegate chats by role, keep internal notes on a
> patient invisible to that patient, and reuse message templates. Founded by Caroline
> Merin (CEO, formerly COO at Rappi), Tom Chokel (CTO, formerly CTO at Modern Fertility)
> and Arela Solis (Head of Operations), the company launched publicly in December 2025
> and operates across Latin America and beyond. Leona ships as an iOS app, an Android app
> and a web client — it does not publish a public developer API.

## Status

- No public API: Leona ships end-user apps. `api.leona.health` is the product's own
  backend and publishes no OpenAPI, no reference documentation and no developer portal.
- No `/.well-known/` discovery documents (see well-known/leona-well-known.yml).
- No first-party SDKs, CLI or client packages published to npm, PyPI or other registries.
